Article IX — Amendments

Sound Legal — Original

The Bylaws may be amended by vote of Owners holding at least sixty percent (60%) of the total votes in the Association, provided all Owners entitled to vote thereon are duly notified of the proposed amendment.

Article IX — Amendments

The Bylaws may be amended by vote of Members holding at least ____% of the total votes in the Association, provided all Members entitled to vote thereon are duly notified of the proposed amendment.

What Changed & Why

Open question: how many votes should it take to change the bylaws? The committee hasn't chosen a number yet. Every option counts all 289 lots, not just the people who return a ballot:

  • 60%: at least 174 lots vote yes. This is the threshold in our current bylaws and the Sound Legal draft.
  • 51%: at least 148 lots vote yes.
  • 40%: at least 116 lots vote yes.
  • 40% with a majority: at least 116 lots vote yes, and the yes votes are a majority of all ballots returned.

A higher bar keeps the bylaws stable. But when turnout is low, even a popular change can fail simply because not enough people voted. A lower bar makes needed updates easier, but lets a smaller group change the rules.

Whatever the number, every member must be notified of a proposed amendment before the vote.
