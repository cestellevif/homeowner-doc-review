Homeowner Document Review

Your Voice Matters

Our community's governing documents — bylaws, covenants, and founding articles — shape how we live together. Homeowner Document Review (HDR) was formed from the original bylaws committee, and we built this website to share what we know.

HDR first set out to continue that original work. A new bylaws committee was formed in June, and it is your current committee. This site was kept for reference and, in November 2026, repurposed to reflect that committee's work. This page was updated later than the rest of the site, once it was no longer the landing page we were directing neighbors to.

We're still neighbors trying to connect with neighbors, and we still want robust discussions about quorum and waivers. Here you'll find the current state of committee work, which is open to all members, alongside the language we're revising and an account of why changes were agreed on or proposed.

Use the feedback buttons to share your thoughts on any clause. We will bring these to our discussions in the bylaws committee.
